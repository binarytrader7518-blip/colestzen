ColestZen alternatives and other options

ColestZen is one option among several. Here are other ways people support healthy cholesterol, and how they compare.

1. Lifestyle changes

Diet, physical activity and weight management have the strongest evidence for healthy cholesterol, according to the CDC. They are the foundation, whatever else you choose. Our food guide is a good place to start.

2. Prescription treatment

If your cholesterol is high, your doctor may recommend medicine. Prescription treatments are tested in clinical trials and monitored by your doctor. A supplement is not a replacement for them.

3. Single-ingredient supplements

Some people prefer single ingredients so they know the exact dose, for example a magnesium or chromium supplement. The trade-off is less convenience than a combined formula. Check the NIH fact sheets for safe intake levels.

4. Other multi-ingredient formulas

Many combined formulas target cholesterol or blood sugar. When comparing them with ColestZen, look at whether doses are disclosed, the length of the guarantee, whether billing is one-time or a subscription, and the cost per day.
