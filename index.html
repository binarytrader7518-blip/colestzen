What is ColestZen?

ColestZen is a capsule dietary supplement created for adults who want to support healthy cholesterol levels as part of their daily routine. Each bottle holds 60 capsules, a 30-day supply at the listed serving of two capsules a day.

The formula pairs traditional botanicals, such as bitter melon, cinnamon, licorice root and yarrow, with micronutrients including vitamin C, vitamin E, magnesium, zinc and chromium. The seller says it is made in the USA and is non-GMO and stimulant-free.

What is ColestZen?

ColestZen is a dietary supplement in capsule form. The seller describes it as a formula for adults who want to support healthy cholesterol and balanced blood sugar levels. It combines nine botanicals with nine vitamins, minerals and nutrients. See the full product overview.

What ingredients are in ColestZen?

The seller lists 18 ingredients. The nine botanicals are bitter melon extract, cinnamon bark, licorice root extract, Gymnema sylvestre leaf, banaba leaf extract, mulberry fruit powder, yarrow herb, cayenne pepper extract and juniper berry powder. The nine micronutrients are L-taurine, alpha lipoic acid, vitamin C, vitamin E, biotin, magnesium, zinc, manganese and chromium. The sales page does not publish the amount of each ingredient, so check the Supplement Facts panel on your bottle.

How do I take ColestZen?

The seller's directions are two capsules once a day, in the morning, with a glass of water. Do not take more than the label directs. How it works has more on fitting it into your routine.
