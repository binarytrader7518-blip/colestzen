Intended purpose

Who ColestZen is made for

The seller describes ColestZen as a next-generation formula for women and men who want natural support for healthy cholesterol, circulation, blood sugar balance and heart health. Its main audience is adults over 35, the age when routine check-ups often start to include cholesterol and blood sugar numbers.

It is a supplement, not a medicine. It is meant to complement a heart-healthy diet, regular activity and the care plan you have agreed with your doctor. The seller itself recommends showing the bottle to your doctor before you start.
